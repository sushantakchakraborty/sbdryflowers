WHO WE ARE

Crafted From Nature, Made With Care.

SB SHOLA WOOD FLOWERS (S.B. Dry Flower) is an established manufacturing business based in West Bengal, dedicated to shaping natural shola plant pith into premium handcrafted floral creations since 2016.

Derived from the spongy, renewable core of the shola plant, every petal and stem is meticulously sculpted by hand. Our floral collections serve florists, interior decorators, event organizers, craft enthusiasts, and wholesale buyers across India with authentic botanical warmth.

NATURAL ADVANTAGES

Why Choose Shola Flowers?

The natural appeal of shola dry flowers lies in their renewable organic origin, lightweight structure, and enduring artisan charm.

100% NATURAL PITH

Harvested sustainably from the organic pith of the shola plant, offering an eco-conscious, lightweight material with authentic botanical feel.

HANDCRAFTED CARE

Every petal is shaped and finished individually by skilled artisans, giving each blossom unique character and tactile refinement.

VERSATILE DÉCOR

Adaptable across interior home décor, wedding floral backdrops, gift packaging, retail displays, and creative craft arrangements.

LONG-LASTING BEAUTY

Unlike fresh florals that fade quickly, properly stored shola flowers maintain their structural integrity and warm aesthetic for years.

MANUFACTURING PROCESS

The Artisan Craft Journey

How raw, spongy shola plant pith is carefully transformed into refined decorative botanical pieces.

01

SELECT

Carefully choosing high-grade, spongy natural shola plant pith reeds for clean texture, light weight, and organic flexibility.

02

CRAFT

Shaping, peeling, slicing, and curving individual petals by hand to form roses, lotus blooms, pine sticks, and delicate floral shapes.

03

ARRANGE

Finishing, binding blooms onto natural stems, quality checking, and securely preparing orders for clients and wholesale dispatches.
